01
Vision
What we're building, and why it matters

Astron Consulting Services processes hundreds of property valuation requests monthly, each routed through a tangled mix of WhatsApp messages, shared spreadsheets, and manual PDF templates. Engineers in the field have no structured way to capture measurements. Admins manually chase status updates. Bank submissions arrive late.

We're building the operational backbone that Astron doesn't have yet — a secure, role-based platform where every case has a single source of truth: from initial intake, through GPS-verified site visits, to automated bank-format PDF reports and billing closure.

Think of it as going from a shared Google Sheet to an operations platform built specifically for how Astron works — without adding complexity, only removing it.
