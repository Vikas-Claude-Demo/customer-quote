01

The vision

Astron Consulting Services processes hundreds of property valuation requests monthly, each routed through a tangled mix of WhatsApp messages, shared spreadsheets, and manual PDF templates. Engineers in the field have no structured way to capture measurements. Admins manually chase status updates. Bank submissions arrive late.

We're building the operational backbone that Astron doesn't have yet — a secure, role-based platform where every case has a single source of truth: from initial intake, through GPS-verified site visits, to automated bank-format PDF reports and billing closure.

Think of it as going from a shared Google Sheet to an operations platform built specifically for how Astron works — without adding complexity, only removing it.

07

Project scope

Role-based auth — Supabase Auth with three roles, RLS-enforced at the database layer
Case management — create, assign, track, and close valuation cases with full history
Bank management — register of empanelled banks with contact and portal details
User management — admin CRUD for engineers and admin accounts; onboarding flow
Mobile-responsive web app — GPS check-in, measurements, boundary data, and structured form capture for field engineers
Photo upload — categorised photo capture per visit (exterior, interior, locality, site)
PDF report generation — automated reports for ICICI & HDFC using react-pdf
Internal ticketing — raise and resolve case queries in-platform; replaces WhatsApp
Financial MIS — revenue tracking, billing status, and performance charts
Admin dashboard — global visibility with Recharts-powered analytics
