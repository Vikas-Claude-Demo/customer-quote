Risk Register

Identified. Mitigated. Owned.

Every risk has a likelihood, an impact, and a written mitigation. The 15% contingency buffer absorbs ~2 weeks of schedule slip.

Ring SDK access delay
MED HIGH
Apply at SOW signing. Fallback: HealthKit bridge (loses native scores but unblocks development).
CGM partnership delayed
MED HIGH
V1 launch can ship without CGM; add in V1.1. HealthKit glucose read provides partial fallback.
Clinical content not ready
MED HIGH
Ship with 2 programs minimum; others in V1.1. Clinical advisor identified by Week 1.
App Store rejection (health)
LOW HIGH
Pre-submission audit in Week 12. Conservative wellness disclaimers throughout app.
HealthKit / HC data gaps
MED MED
Graceful degradation — hide empty cards; "connect a device" prompt instead of crashes.
LLM costs exceed budget
LOW MED
Hard token caps + daily spend limits + Redis cache. Anthropic primary, OpenAI fallback only.
